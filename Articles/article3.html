2026-10-5

Hosoi is on the Horse

(Under construction, only little part of it.)

Hosoi, President of NuttyShell, returned victorious from Ulaanbaatar.

The Core Team asked the artist syrup chan to paint an oil portrait titled "Hosoi on the Horse" as a gift, showing President Hosoi galloping across Mongolia, unstoppable. Reluctantly, syrup chan accepted the commission under duress.

When the painting was finished, a NuttyShell Core Member came to inspect it and was shocked: it showed a group of people happily enjoying a banquet, with a beautiful view of Ulaanbaatar at night outside the window.

"What is this? Who are all these people?!" the Core Member asked angrily.

"President Hosoi's teammates," syrup chan replied.

"And who else?!"

"The other contestants."

"But where is President Hosoi?"

"Hosoi is on the Horse," syrup chan replied.
